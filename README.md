# Fold City

An endless neon night city that folds up into a tube around you while you fly through it. About 1,500 instanced buildings and 3,200 cars, bent in the vertex shader.

Live: https://saksham.digital/fold-city/

Drag to look, W/S for speed, A/D to slide, look up to climb, scroll or F to fold. One HTML file, three.js r128.
